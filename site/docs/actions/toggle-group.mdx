A toggle group holds the value of its [Toggles](/docs/actions/toggle): the list of the pressed
ones' `value`s. Without `multiple` at most one is pressed, and pressing it again releases it (a
choice that must always have one value is a [Segmented Control](/docs/fields/segmented-control)).
The group is one tab stop: the arrows move between its toggles (roving focus, `loopFocus`,
swapped in RTL), and Space or Enter presses.

## Composition

`ToggleGroup` draws nothing of its own: an inline row (or a column with
`orientation="vertical"`) of `Toggle`s a gap apart, each with its Clickable surface and the
`current` mark when pressed. A row of buttons that share one border is a button group, which has
no behaviour of its own and is not this component.

## Bases

Both bases' `ToggleGroup` behave the same. Base UI's `onValueChange` details can be cancelled; on
Fragiola Primitives they cannot. Inside a [Toolbar](/docs/actions/toolbar), Fragiola Primitives
reports the toolbar's `data-orientation` on the group (the toolbar's keys apply), Base UI its own.
