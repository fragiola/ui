A checkbox group holds the value of its [Checkboxes](/docs/fields/choice): the list of the
checked ones' `value`s (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`). Give it `allValues` and put a
`<Checkbox.Root parent />` in it: the parent checks or clears every checkbox, and while only some
are checked it shows the mixed mark — the choice family's dash on the `checked` fill
(`data-indeterminate`).

## Composition

`CheckboxGroup` draws nothing: a column of `Checkbox`es, each in a `Field.ChoiceRoot` with its
label, a gap apart like a `RadioGroup`'s. Name the group with `aria-labelledby` (the parent's
label, as in the example) or `aria-label`.

## Bases

Both bases' `CheckboxGroup` and the checkbox's `parent` behave the same.
