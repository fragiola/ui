A number field keeps a number, not text: `value` and `onValueChange` hold a `number` (or `null`
while empty), and `format` shows it for the locale (`{ style: "percent" }`, a currency, units).
It steps by `step` with ArrowUp and ArrowDown (Shift for `largeStep`, Alt for `smallStep`), by
`largeStep` with Page Up and Page Down, to `min` and `max` with Home and End, with the Increment and
Decrement buttons (held to repeat), with the wheel when `allowWheelScrub` is set, and by dragging a
ScrubArea sideways. For a bare numeric input without steppers there is `Numeric` in
[Input](/docs/fields/input).

## The field's box

The control is only its middle (rule 3), so a number field is written like any field:

- **Group** is the box, `field.row` — the stroke, the radius, the focus ring;
- **Input** is the bare control, `field.control`, inside a `Field.Body`, with tabular numbers;
- **Decrement** and **Increment** are field addons (`field.addon`): the `soft` adornment, here a
  button. Their side is `data-side`, set by `side` (`inline-start` and `inline-end` by default),
  so both may sit at the end, as the discount does;
- **ScrubArea** wraps the label in a `cursor-ew-resize` area, and **ScrubAreaCursor** is the
  cursor shown while dragging.

The steppers are named "Decrease" and "Increase" unless you give an `aria-label`.

## Bases

The parts and the props are the same on both bases. Where they differ by design: the Input is a
`spinbutton` with `aria-valuenow` on Fragiola Primitives, a textbox described as a number field on
Base UI; Base UI's `allowOutOfRange` is Fragiola Primitives' `clampOnBlur` (its inverse); a
change cannot be cancelled on Fragiola Primitives.
