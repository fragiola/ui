A color wheel picks a colour's hue on a ring. Its thumb is an APG slider over 0–360°: a press
on the ring moves it there and drags it, the arrows step it by a degree (Shift and Page Up /
Page Down by 15°) and wrap past 360°, Home and End go to 0° and 359°. The ring is physical: a
wheel has no reading direction, so RTL changes nothing. `outerRadius` and `innerRadius` (px,
100 and 75 by default) size the ring; the primitive sizes the box from them. `colorSpace` is
`hsl` or `hsb`; `name` submits the colour as hex.

Name the thumb (`aria-label="Hue"`). `ColorWheel.Template.Simple` is the ring and its thumb, named
"Hue" unless you give `aria-label`. Inside a [Color Picker](/docs/color/color-picker), a wheel with
neither `value` nor `defaultValue` **binds** to the picker's colour.

## The look

It composes the `color` family:

- the **Track** is `color.wheelTrack`: the primitive's conic gradient of every hue
  (`--color-wheel-track-background`, 0° at the top, clockwise), masked inside the inner radius
  (`--color-wheel-inner-radius`) so only the ring shows. The ground is the colour being picked,
  never a palette role.
- the **Thumb** is `color.wheelThumb`: the knob with the colour inside and a `contrast` hairline,
  as every colour thumb; it is the slider itself, so its ring is the standalone `focus`.

The ring's size is the primitive's pixel option: it does not follow the density axis.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no color wheel, so this is one shared file
(`ui/color-wheel.tsx`) that wraps Fragiola Primitives' `ColorWheel` on **both** bases, and the item
depends on `@fragiola/primitives-react@0.3.0` on both. Name its thumb with `aria-label` or
`aria-labelledby` on either base: it is no `Field` control.
