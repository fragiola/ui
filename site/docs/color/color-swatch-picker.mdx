A color swatch picker chooses one colour from a palette. It is a listbox whose options take the
focus: Tab enters at the chosen option (else the first), Left and Right move along a row and wrap
(swapped in RTL), Up and Down move to the nearest option of the row above or below (the rows are
the ones your layout wraps into), Home and End go to the ends, and Space, Enter or a press choose.
A focus move never chooses. An option is chosen when its colour is the value's, whatever the
space. `name` submits the chosen colour as hex.

Each `Item` takes a `value` (its colour); its content is a [Color Swatch](/docs/color/color-swatch)
of that value unless you give one, and the swatch's name is the option's name, so name it
(`aria-label="Ocean"`). Name the list with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
`ColorSwatchPicker.Template.Simple` renders an option per colour of `colors` (`{ value, label }`
names one). Inside a [Color Picker](/docs/color/color-picker), a swatch picker with neither `value`
nor `defaultValue` **binds** to the picker's colour.

## The look

It composes the `color` family and the foundation:

- the **Root** is `color.swatchPicker`, a wrapping row with a 4px gap;
- an **Item** is `color.swatchItem`, a 2px frame around its swatch. The primitive marks the chosen
  option `data-selected`; the item adds `data-checked` from its state, so the chosen one is the
  foundation's `checked` (a `ring` frame: "on" is `ring`, on every palette), the others take the
  `hover` fill, and the focus is the standalone ring outside it.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no swatch picker, so this is one shared file
(`ui/color-swatch-picker.tsx`) that wraps Fragiola Primitives' `ColorSwatchPicker` on **both**
bases, and the item depends on `@fragiola/primitives-react@0.2.0` on both. It follows the direction
of the base's `DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.

On Base UI, the registry's `Field` is Base UI's, and a Fragiola Primitives control does not join
it: name the list with `aria-labelledby` (the label's `id`) or `aria-label`, as the example does,
and pass `name` and `disabled` to the Root. On Fragiola Primitives the picker is also a `Field`
control.

## Parts

| Part | Source |
|---|---|

| `Item` | primitive `ColorSwatchPicker.Item` + `color.swatchItem()` (`data-checked` from its state); a ColorSwatch of its value by default |
