A color area picks two channels of one colour at once: saturation across and brightness up by
default, the square under a hue slider. Its accessible model is **two APG sliders**, one hidden
native range input per axis inside the thumb, so a screen reader meets two named sliders with
their values. A press on the area moves the thumb there and drags it; either input moves both
axes (Left and Right move `x`, Up and Down, Page Up and Page Down move `y`, Shift takes the
large step, Home and End set `x` to its ends), and the focus follows the axis a key moved. In
RTL, `x` runs from the right. `xChannel`, `yChannel` and `colorSpace` choose the axes (red and
green in rgb, saturation and lightness in hsl, ...); a change reports the colour in that space.
Each input's `name` submits its channel.

Name the area (`aria-label`: it is a `group`) and each input (`aria-label="Saturation"`).
`ColorArea.Template.Simple` is the area, its thumb and both inputs named after their channels
(saturation and brightness by default). Inside a [Color Picker](/docs/color/color-picker), an area
with neither `value` nor `defaultValue` **binds** to the picker's colour.

## The look

It composes the `color` family:

- the **Root** is `color.area`: the primitive's two background layers and their blend mode
  (`--color-area-background`, `--color-area-background-blend-mode`). The ground is the colour
  being picked, never a palette role. A hairline inside its edge keeps a white corner apart from a
  white floor. Its size is yours (`h-48 w-full` by default; `className="size-40"`).
- the **Thumb** is `color.areaThumb`: the foundation's `knob` (the palette's `base` over a light
  shadow) with the colour inside it and a `contrast` hairline around it, so it reads over white,
  black and saturated colours. Its focus ring is `focusNested`, drawn while one of its inputs
  has the keyboard focus.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no color area, so this is one shared file
(`ui/color-area.tsx`) that wraps Fragiola Primitives' `ColorArea` on **both** bases, and the item
depends on `@fragiola/primitives-react@0.2.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too (`lib/primitives-backed.tsx`), and `dir="rtl"` without one.

The area is no `Field` control on either base (its two values have two names): name it and its
inputs with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
