A color slider edits one channel of a colour: `channel` is `hue`, `saturation`, `lightness`,
`brightness`, `red`, `green`, `blue` or `alpha`, and its range and steps are the channel's (0–360°
by 1 for a hue, 0–100% for a saturation, 0–255 for a red, 0–100% by 1% for an alpha). The thumb
is an APG slider: the arrows step it, Shift and Page Up / Page Down take the large step, Home and
End go to the ends, a press on the track moves it there and drags it. In RTL a horizontal slider
runs from the right; `orientation="vertical"` runs it from the bottom. `colorSpace` chooses the
space the channel is edited in (a change reports the colour in it), and `name` submits the colour
as hex.

`Label` names the group and the thumb, and a press on it focuses the thumb; `Output` writes the
channel's value (`120°`, `50%`). `ColorSlider.Template.Simple` is the label (the channel's name
unless you give `label`), the value, the track and the thumb. Inside a
[Color Picker](/docs/color/color-picker), a slider with neither `value` nor `defaultValue`
**binds** to the picker's colour.

## The look

It composes the `color` family:

- the **Track** is `color.sliderTrack`: the primitive's gradient from the channel's minimum to its
  maximum (`--color-slider-track-background`), the colour's other channels kept. The ground is
  the colour being picked, never a palette role. Under it is the checkerboard, seen only where
  the gradient is transparent: the alpha channel.
- the **Thumb** is `color.sliderThumb`: the knob with the colour inside and a `contrast` hairline,
  as every colour thumb; it is the slider itself, so its ring is the standalone `focus`.
- the **Label** is the field's label type, the **Output** secondary text.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI's Slider edits a number, not a colour's channel, and Base UI has no
color slider, so this is one shared file (`ui/color-slider.tsx`) that wraps Fragiola Primitives'
`ColorSlider` on **both** bases, and the item depends on `@fragiola/primitives-react@0.3.0` on
both. It follows the direction of the base's `DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"`
without one. Its `Label` names it on both bases.

## Parts

| Part | Source |
|---|---|
| `Root` | primitive `ColorSlider.Root` + `color.slider()`: the label and the value on one line, the track under them |
| `Label` | primitive `ColorSlider.Label` rendering `Text.Label` + `field.label()` |
| `Output` | primitive `ColorSlider.Output`, secondary text |
| `Track` | primitive `ColorSlider.Track` + `color.sliderTrack()` |
| `Thumb` | primitive `ColorSlider.Thumb` + `color.sliderThumb()` |
| `Template.Simple` | the label, the value, the track and the thumb |
