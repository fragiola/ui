A calendar view lets a reader go far in time without paging month by month: the heading is a
button that goes one view up — days, then months, then years — and a choice goes back down: a
year shows its months, a month its days. A view only navigates; the date is chosen in the day
grid. After each switch the new view's focused cell takes the focus. It goes inside a Calendar,
Range Calendar or Multiple Calendar Root, inline or in a picker's popup, where Escape still
closes the popup in every view and reopening shows the day view again.

`CalendarView.Template.Simple` is the three views: the day grid and the
[Month](/docs/dates/month-calendar) and [Year](/docs/dates/year-calendar) calendars, each headed
by the Trigger holding its heading. The Trigger is `calendar.viewTrigger`: the heading's type with
a chevron, the `hover` fill and the `focus` ring; in the year view it is disabled and stays a
heading.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no calendar view, so this is one shared file (`ui/calendar-view.tsx`) that wraps
Fragiola Primitives' `CalendarView` on **both** bases, and the item depends on
`@fragiola/primitives-react@0.3.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.


## Parts

| Part | Source |
|---|---|
| `Root` | primitive `CalendarView.Root`: the view state, no element; children see `{ view }` |
| `Trigger` | primitive `CalendarView.Trigger` + `calendar.viewTrigger`, with a chevron; put the view's `Heading` in it, rendered as a `span` |
| `Template.Simple` | the three views; `view`, `defaultView` and `onViewChange` are forwarded |
