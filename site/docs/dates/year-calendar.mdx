A year calendar chooses a year. A page holds 12 years starting at a multiple of 12 (2016 to
2027 holds 2026), so paging never shifts the rows; the heading is the page's span, from `Intl`.
Its keys, bounds and cells are the [Month Calendar](/docs/dates/month-calendar)'s, by years.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no year calendar, so this is one shared file (`ui/year-calendar.tsx`) that wraps
Fragiola Primitives' `YearCalendar` on **both** bases, and the item depends on
`@fragiola/primitives-react@0.2.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.


## Parts

The [Month Calendar](/docs/dates/month-calendar)'s parts, named `year-calendar-*`, with its own
`Root` (primitive `YearCalendar.Root`) and `Template.Simple`; the page buttons are named
"Previous years", "Next years".
