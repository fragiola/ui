A month range calendar chooses a first and a last month, as a [Range
Calendar](/docs/dates/range-calendar) chooses dates: the first choice starts the range, the
months between preview it, the second ends it. The ends are `checked` and the months between are
the Calendar's band, rounded at a row's ends, logical in RTL. Keys and bounds are the [Month
Calendar](/docs/dates/month-calendar)'s.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no month range calendar, so this is one shared file (`ui/month-range-calendar.tsx`) that wraps
Fragiola Primitives' `MonthRangeCalendar` on **both** bases, and the item depends on
`@fragiola/primitives-react@0.3.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.


## Parts

The [Month Calendar](/docs/dates/month-calendar)'s parts, named `month-range-calendar-*`, with its
own `Root` (primitive `MonthRangeCalendar.Root`) and `Template.Simple`.
