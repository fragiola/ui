A range calendar chooses a start and an end. The first choice (a press, Enter or Space) starts
the range, and the dates between it and the pointer or the focus preview it; the second choice
ends it, swapped when it comes first, and `onValueChange` fires once with `{ start, end }`.
Escape cancels a started range before an enclosing popup or dialog closes. Its keys, bounds and
locale are the [Calendar](/docs/dates/calendar)'s.

## The range

It is the `calendar` family's: the start and the end are `checked` (the `ring` box); the dates
between are one `soft` band drawn behind the boxes, from the start's centre to the end's, and
rounded at a week's ends. The band uses logical insets (`start-1/2`, `rounded-s-*`), so in a
right-to-left page it joins the same way, mirrored — the example's `rtl` row. While a new range is
being chosen, the committed band hides and the preview (half of `soft`) follows the pointer; the
committed ends keep their box until the new range is chosen.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no range calendar, so this is one shared file (`ui/range-calendar.tsx`) that wraps
Fragiola Primitives' `RangeCalendar` on **both** bases, and the item depends on
`@fragiola/primitives-react@0.2.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.


## Parts

The [Calendar](/docs/dates/calendar)'s parts, named `range-calendar-*`, with its own `Root`
(primitive `RangeCalendar.Root`) and `Template.Simple`.
