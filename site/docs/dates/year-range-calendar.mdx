A year range calendar chooses a first and a last year on pages of 12 years, as a [Month Range
Calendar](/docs/dates/month-range-calendar) chooses months: the ends are `checked`, the years
between the Calendar's band.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no year range calendar, so this is one shared file (`ui/year-range-calendar.tsx`) that wraps
Fragiola Primitives' `YearRangeCalendar` on **both** bases, and the item depends on
`@fragiola/primitives-react@0.2.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.


## Parts

The [Month Calendar](/docs/dates/month-calendar)'s parts, named `year-range-calendar-*`, with its
own `Root` (primitive `YearRangeCalendar.Root`) and `Template.Simple`; the page buttons are named
"Previous years", "Next years".
