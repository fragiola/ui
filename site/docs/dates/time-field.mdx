A time field types a time as the [Date Field](/docs/dates/date-field) types a date: hours and
minutes (`granularity="second"` adds seconds) and, on the locale's 12-hour clock, the day period
(type the first letter of AM or PM). `hourCycle` forces 12 or 24 hours. The value is a
`{ hour, minute, second, millisecond }`; `min` and `max` make a time outside them invalid.
Its segments are the Date Field's: the focused one `highlighted`, a placeholder secondary.

## Bases

**Primitives-backed.** Base UI has no time field, so this is one shared file (`ui/time-field.tsx`) that wraps
Fragiola Primitives' `TimeField` on **both** bases, and the item depends on
`@fragiola/primitives-react@0.2.0` on both. It follows the direction of the base's
`DirectionProvider` on Base UI too, and `dir="rtl"` without one.

On Base UI, the registry's `Field` is Base UI's, and a Fragiola Primitives control does not join
it: name the group with `aria-labelledby` (the label's `id`, as the example does) or `aria-label`,
and pass `name`, `disabled` and `required` to the Root. On Fragiola Primitives it is also a
`Field` control: the label names the group, a press on the label focuses the first segment, and
the Field's description, `disabled` and validation reach it.

**Invalid.** The field's own invalid state (`data-invalid` on its parts) turns its segments
`palette-danger` by inheritance; the row's stroke follows when the `Field` itself is invalid —
pass `invalid` to `Field.Root`, which works on both bases.

## Parts

The [Date Field](/docs/dates/date-field)'s parts, named `time-field-*`, with its own `Root`
(primitive `TimeField.Root`).
