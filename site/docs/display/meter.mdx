A meter shows where a value sits in a known range: storage against a quota, seats against a plan,
a score. It is the `meter` role, not a task's progress (that is [Progress](/docs/display/progress)).
`value`, `min` and `max` place it, and `format` writes its value for the locale.

## The track family

`Meter`, `Progress` and `Slider` share the `track` family: `track.rail` (the rail, `line`) and
`track.fill` (the fill, `ring`, the colour of every "on" mark). The fill grows from the inline
start and inverts under RTL. The label is a field label and the value secondary text, beside it.

## Bases

Both bases' `Meter` have the same parts and behave the same.
