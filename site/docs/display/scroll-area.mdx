## The foundation

The box adds no role, key or focus stop of its own: the content scrolls natively and the browser's scrollbars are replaced by parts that a pointer drags (the thumb) and presses (the track).

- The track is transparent and thin, shown while the pointer is over the box or it scrolls (`data-hovering`, `data-scrolling`).
- The thumb is a pill in `line`, the foundation's `rail`: the ground of every track, which stands out on any floor. The `knob` (`base` over a shadow) would vanish on the floor it sits on.
- When the viewport is a tab stop — its content overflows and holds nothing focusable — its ring is `focusInset`, drawn inside since the box clips it. Name it then (`aria-label`, which `Template.Simple` turns into a named region).

`ScrollArea.Template.Simple` is the common box: the viewport, its content, both scrollbars and the corner. Give it a height (or a `max-h-*`) and a `className` for the frame.

## Bases

The component is Base UI's Scroll Area on the Base UI base and Fragiola Primitives' on the default one: the same parts, attributes and CSS variables. Parts, classes and `data-slot` names are the same on both bases.

## Parts

| Part | Source |

| `Viewport` | primitive `Viewport` + `foundation.focusInset()` |

| `Scrollbar` | primitive `Scrollbar` + its `Thumb` (`foundation.rail()`) |
| `Corner` | primitive `Corner` |
| `Template.Simple` | the whole box |
